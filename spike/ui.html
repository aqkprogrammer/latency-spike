Endpointer dashboard

This measures one thing: when a caller stops talking, how long before the agent works out they've finished. Get it wrong in one direction and you cut people off mid-sentence. Wrong in the other and every exchange has a second of dead air. This page shows both failures at once, across six test utterances.

What you're looking at

fixed — the baseline

Wait N milliseconds of silence, then assume they're done

What almost every voice stack ships with, and what we're trying to beat. It has no idea what the caller was saying, so a thinking pause looks identical to the end of a sentence.

fused — ours

Silence, plus falling pitch, plus whether the sentence is finished

Reads the transcript as it arrives. If the words end on “to” or “aur”, it waits however long that takes. If they end on “haan”, it fires almost immediately.

green The caller is speaking. Gaps between blocks are silences.
dashed Ground truth — the last moment there is any speech in the file. Firing left of this line means you cut the caller off.
red mark Where the fixed timeout decided the caller had finished.
green mark Where the fused endpointer decided. The number in brackets is milliseconds after the caller actually stopped — smaller is better, negative is a cut.
